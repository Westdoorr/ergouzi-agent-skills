# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, review ideas rather than people,
welcome good-faith questions, disclose conflicts of interest, and protect the
privacy and security of users and maintainers.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, credential exposure, deceptive
security claims, and intentionally unsafe contributions are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to an Aiman Labs maintainer. Maintainers may
edit or remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or refer
serious threats to the relevant platform or authorities. Reports are handled as
confidentially as practical.

This policy applies to repository discussions, reviews, community channels, and
public representation of the project.
